Decoupling Intrinsic Molecular Efficacy from Platform Effects: An Interpretable 
Machine Learning Framework for Unbiased Perovskite Passivator Discovery 
This study establishes an interpretable machine learning framework that disentangles the 
intrinsic molecular efficacy of passivators from experimental platform effects—enabling 
unbiased, high-throughput discovery of effective perovskite surface modifiers. By integrating 
a data-driven asymptotic saturation model with uncertainty-quantified virtual screening across 
121 million molecules, we identify five dual-functional candidates with strong predicted 
performance. This closed-loop strategy transforms materials design from empirical trial-and-
error to a rational, mechanism-guided paradigm, accelerating the discovery of next-generation 
perovskite solar cell passivator.
