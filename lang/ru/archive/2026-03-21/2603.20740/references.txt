The overall scheme of observing lines in emission and ab-
sorption is discussed in, e.g., Khabibullin & Churazov (2019),
Churazov et al. (2023). One needs to use telescopes with a large
grapstomeasurediffuseemissionandobserveone(orpreferably

promising would be using a telescope that combines these two
properties, such asLEM(Kraft et al. 2022).
