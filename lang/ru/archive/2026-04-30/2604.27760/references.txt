excitation of chlorophylla. (B) Cells within focus are segmented using a Voronoi-Otsu method. (C) Colony envelope is obtained
using binary closing operation. (D) Average tensile strength increases with the average nearest-neighbor distance (NND) (Pearson
correlation,r(24) = 0.47,p= 0.02). (E) Conversely, the average tensile strength of the colonies decreases with the area fraction
between cells and the colony (Pearson correlation,r(24) = -0.45,p= 0.02). (F) No significant correlation was found between the
average tensile strength and colony diameter (Pearson correlation,r(24) = -0.16,p= 0.43), (G) as well as with the cell diameter
(Pearson correlation,r(24) = 0.17,p= 0.40). Small symbols indicate the average tensile strength of individual colonies, while large
symbols indicate the average of treatments. Number of colonies = 26
