Materials and Methods
Samples fabrication
The heterostructure (31, 39) is grown using molecular beam epitaxty on a GaAs (100) semi-
insulating wafer. On top of the heterostructure, the following layers reside (in the order of growth):
Al0.8Ga0.2As sacrificial layer (400 nm), AlAs/GaAs superlattice (42.5 nm, alternating 4 AlAs/8
GaAs monolayers), Si𝛿-doping (1.5×1011 cm-2), AlAs/GaAs superlattice (33.5 nm), GaAs quantum
well (13 nm), AlAs/GaAs superlattice (33.5 nm), Si𝛿-doping (1.5×10 11 cm-2), AlAs/GaAs
superlattice (33.5 nm), GaAs (5 nm), Si𝛿-doping (2.5×10 11 cm-2), GaAs (5 nm). The layers
above the sacrifical layer form a 166 nm-thick membrane with a two-dimensional electron gas in
the middle of its thickness.
Shallow mesa and Ohmic contacts are patterned using mask-free optical lithography (direct
writer). The mesa is wet etched in H2SO4:H2O2:H2O 1:8:80 solution to the depth of 80 nm. Among
others, the shallow mesa is used to form the oscillation detectors. The Ohmic contacts consist
of Au/Ge eutectic alloy, with Ni adhesion layer. The geometry of the resonators is defined using
electron beam lithography with subsequent anisotropic etching in BCl 3:Ar plasma to the depth
of approximately 180 nm. After that, the sacrificial layer is selectively removed from beneath the
resonators in HF:H 2O 1:10 solution. After rinsing in deionized water, the samples are dried in
critical carbon dioxide to avoid capillary forces.
Measurement setup
The measurements are performed in a dry He-4 cryostat with a variable temperature insert equipped
with a superconducting magnet. During the measurements, the samples are placed in vacuum to
avoid damping of mechanical oscillations due to environment (40).
