SM3. Domain-Specific Language Example.Complementing the compress-
ible Euler specification in the main article, Listing 2 gives the complete DSL specifi-
cation of the compressible Navier–Stokes–Fourier system declared in SI units. Two
non-dimensionalization steps are declared. First, characteristic references (the density
and the gas constant) eliminate the mass and temperature base dimensions, from
which the compiler derives every lattice grid-scaling exponent automatically, with the
dynamic viscosity (Pas) and thermal conductivity (W m−1 K−1) reducing to the diffu-
sive (length2/time) scaling. Second, the realistic-SI magnitudes (density1.225 kg/m3,
sound speed340 m/s) are declared as characteristic symbols, and the manufactured so-
lution is then authored as (scale)×(dimensionless), so the value non-dimensionalization
divides the scales out through the products and places the working point at theO(1)
lattice scale.
Listing 2: Formulation of the Navier–Stokes–Fourier equations in SI units.
1from pde2lbm import *
2from sympy . physics . units import ( length , time , velocity , mass ,
3temperature , energy , pressure , power , momentum )
4
5cns = ConservationLaws ( dim =2)
6
7# 1. States and parameters with named SI dimensions
8rho = cns . register_state (" rho ", dim = mass / length **3)# density
9rhou = cns . register_state (" rhou ", shape =2 , dim = momentum / length **3)
10E = cns . register_state ("E", dim = energy / length **3)# energy
11
12gamma = cns . register_parameter (" gamma ")
13mu = cns . register_parameter ("mu", dim = pressure * time )# Pa s
14K = cns . register_parameter ("K", dim = power /( length * temperature ))# W/(m K)
15R = cns . register_parameter ("R", dim = energy /( mass * temperature ))# gas const
16
17# 2a. Eliminate mass and temperature base dimensions
18cns . set_characteristic (mass , rho )
19cns . set_characteristic ( temperature , R)
20
21# 2b. Value non - dimensionalization : realistic SI working scales as
characteristic symbols
22rho0 = cns . characteristic_symbol (mass , 1.225 , name =" rho0 ")# air at ~1 atm
23c_s = cns . characteristic_symbol ( velocity , 340.0 , name =" c_s ")# sound speed
24
25# 3. Dependent variables
26u = cns . register_derived ("u", rhou / rho )# registers u_0 , u_1
27p = ( gamma - 1) *(E - 0.5* rho *u. dot (u))
28T = p/( rho *R)# temperature
29
30# 4. Newtonian stress + Fourier flux
31gu = cgrad (u)
32tau = mu *( gu + gu.T - (2.0/3.0) * trace (gu)* eye (2) )
33q = -K* cgrad (T)
34
35# 5. Equation system
36cns . add ([
37Eq(dt( rho ) + div ( rhou ), 0) ,
38Eq(dt( rhou ) + div ( outer (rhou ,u) + p* eye (2) - tau ), 0) ,
39Eq(dt(E) + div ((E+p)*u - tau *u + q), 0) ,
40])
41
42# 6. Generate OpenLB solver
43cns . compile ( class_name =" CompressibleNSF ")

SM5. Method of Manufactured Solutions (MMS) Exact Fields.In this
appendix, we document the analytical expressions of theMethod of Manufactured
Solutions(MMS) exact fields (Qexact) for all twelve verified physical PDE targets.
The volume forcing terms are derived from these fields symbolically at compile time
as the continuous residualSmms = ∂tQexact + ∇ ·Φ(Qexact) − S(Qexact). We list the
conserved macroscopic components. The gradient-tracker components carried by some
systems are initialized from the spatial derivatives of these fields and are omitted here.
Cases declared with SI-valued scales author their fields as (scale)×(dimensionless),
and the value non-dimensionalization divides the scales out to reach theO(1)lattice
working point tabulated per case.
SM5.1. Design Principles for the Manufactured Solutions.
1. Smoothness.All fields are C∞ trigonometric–exponential profiles, so the
measured order reflects the truncation error of the scheme.
2. Large amplitudes.Fluctuations reach a finite fraction of the mean state, so
the nonlinearities are exercised.
3. Independent modes.Fields coupled by a constitutive law carry independent
fluctuation modes rather than being slaved to one another.
4. Broken axis symmetry.Per-axis wavenumbers, Mach numbers, or phase
patterns are distinct.
5. Off-resonance forcing.Temporal frequencies avoid the free eigenfrequencies,
and the evaluation instant falls at a generic phase.
6. Involutions by construction.Differential constraints carried by the physics are
satisfied analytically by the exact fields.
