1. Particle tracking velocimetry
We use solidiﬁedD2 particles as tracers to visualize quan-
tized vortices in He II. The D2 tracers are generated by
mixing 1% D2 gas with 99% He gas at room tempera-
ture using a custom-built gas-handling system (see Sup-
plementary Fig. 1 for a schematic), and then injecting
the mixture into the experimental cell. The ﬂow rate is
regulated by a needle valve, while a computer-controlled
solenoid valve sets the injection duration. For each in-
jection, the solenoid valve is opened for 30–40 s, and this
sequence is repeated three times at a repetition rate of
0.02 Hz. Upon injection, theD2 gas solidiﬁes into ice
particles. To promote vortex generation, in some runs
we turn on a chip-resistor heater (60 Ω in He II) by ap-
plying a step-voltage pulse of 8 V amplitude and 2 ms
duration.
