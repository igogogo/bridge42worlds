Artificial Intelligence Discovered 247 New Cosmic Lenses express

Original: "Searching for Galaxy Cluster-Scale Strong lenses from the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys"
· Zhejian Zhang, Nan Li, Shude Mao, Hu Zou, Zizhao He, Mingxiang Fu, Shenzhe Cui
arXiv:2601.11054 · 2026-01-16 · CC BY 4.0 · 1 min
Galaxies
A neural network identified 247 previously unknown gravitational lenses—nature's telescopes that bend light from distant galaxies.
1

Just one galaxy cluster can act as a natural telescope, showing us galaxies we'd otherwise never see. Artificial intelligence found 247 such 'lenses'—this will help unravel the mysteries of dark matter.

🎯 Gravitational lensing sometimes produces 'Einstein rings'—perfect circles of light when the lens, source, and observer align just right. In these rings, you can even make out individual star clusters in distant galaxies.
