Molecule as a Wire: Chemistry Dictates the Current's Path express

Original: "Fundamental Relation between Conductance of Biomolecules and the Fukui Function"
· Gabor Vattay
arXiv:2607.13309 · 2026-07-14 · CC BY · 1 min
Soft Condensed Matter Mesoscale Materials Biological Physics q-bio.BM
The most chemically active spots in a molecule are the best at conducting electricity.
1

It turns out that in molecules, the stickiest spots for electrons line up with the paths current flows. This discovery links chemical activity to conductivity, and scientists already foresee using drugs to control electrical signals in proteins and building bioelectronic chips on that basis.

🎯 Enzyme active sites are the hottest 'hot spots' for conductivity: almost all the current flows through them.

🎬 Protein chips aren't new to science fiction: the biocomputers in William Gibson's "Neuromancer" worked exactly that way.
