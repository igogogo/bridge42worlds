Invisibility Cloak from Multilayered Metal express

Original: "Material- and geometry-independent multishell cloaking device"
· Pattabhiraju C. Mundru, Venkatesh Pappakrishnan, Dentcho A. Genov
arXiv:2606.21825 · 2026-06-20 · CC BY 4.0 · 1 min
Optics
Alternating layers of metal and insulator conceal objects of any shape.
1

A multilayered shell of metal and dielectric turns any object into an 'invisibility cloak', reducing light scattering by 1000 times. The technology works for objects of any shape and material, bringing us closer to compact optical invisibility cloaks.

🎯 In nature, glass sponges use the same principle – their transparent bodies are nearly invisible in water.

🎬 The fantastical invisibility cloak takes on real features in these laboratory studies.
