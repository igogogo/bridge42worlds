Abstract

Starlight spread into a rainbow is a star's footprint on a telescope image. Among millions of such footprints, only a few are odd—like spotting a bird's track among animal prints. Spec-o3 is a digital tracker. It learns from masters: first copying their actions, then practicing on its own, making mistakes and correcting them. On LAMOST telescope images, the program's accuracy nearly tripled—from 28 to 77 out of 100. Its instincts work on other telescopes too: SDSS, DESI.
