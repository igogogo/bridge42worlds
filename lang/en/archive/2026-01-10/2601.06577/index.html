Тесное танго звёзд: двойная природа сверхновой SN 2018gj ✦

Original: "A binary merger product as the direct progenitor of a Type II-P supernova"
· Zexi Niu, Ning-Chen Sun, Emmanouil Zapartas, Dimitris Souropanis, Yingzhen Cui, Justyn R. Maund, JeffJ. Andrews, Max M. Briel, Morgan Fraser, Seth Gossage, Matthias U. arXiv:2601.06577 · 2026-01-10 · CC BY · 1 min
High Energy

Abstract

Supernova 2018gj, a type II-P (explosion of a massive star with a long plateau on its light curve), behaved oddly: despite a similar temperature and luminosity to other red supergiant progenitors, it showed a shortened plateau and resided in a very old stellar environment. Modeling of binary system evolution showed that such features can only be explained if the progenitor formed from the merger of two stars in a tight pair—giving the star a different internal structure and a longer life. This discovery provides the first convincing evidence that the most common type of supernovae can originate not only from single stars but also from binary systems.
