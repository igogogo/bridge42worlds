A Star with a Secret: 70-Day Plateau Betrays a Supernova's Binary Past ✦

Original: "A binary merger product as the direct progenitor of a Type II-P supernova"
· Zexi Niu, Ning-Chen Sun, Emmanouil Zapartas, Dimitris Souropanis, Yingzhen Cui, Justyn R. Maund, JeffJ. Andrews, Max M. Briel, Morgan Fraser, Seth Gossage, Matthias U. Supernova SN 2018gj provides the first direct evidence that its progenitor, a red supergiant, was born from a stellar merger. A short plateau and ancient surroundings spilled the secret.

🎯 A single star of that mass lives 30 million years. The environment of SN 2018gj is older than 40 million. The merger gave it an extra 10 million—and gave away the secret.
