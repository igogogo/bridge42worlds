The Baikal-GVD telescope studied 92 objects in search of neutrinos but found no convincing signals.
1

Deep in Lake Baikal, a giant telescope hunts for neutrinos — ghost particles. For five years, it stared at 92 cosmic objects, finding only murky hints. But now scientists know exactly where neutrinos aren't — and that's a step toward future discoveries.

🎯 Billions of neutrinos pass through your palm every second, but over a lifetime, only one or two will ever touch an atom in your body.
