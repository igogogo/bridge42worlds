Neural Network Drew Maps of 639,000 Galaxies express

Original: "Deep Learning Segmentation of Spiral Arms and Bars for 600,000 Galaxies in DESI"
· Ashley Spindler, Mike Walmsley, Karen L. Masters, Tobias Géron, Izzy L. Garland, B. D. Simmons, Jürgen J. Popp
arXiv:2606.16507 · 2026-06-15 · CC BY 4.0 · 1 min
Galaxies Instrumentation
The algorithm turned images of distant galaxies into detailed maps: marking spiral arms, central bars, and unexpectedly discovered rings.
Abstract

Imagine you have images of nearly 640,000 distant galaxies. Astronomers trained a neural network that, like a skilled gardener, recognizes branches—spiral arms—and trunks—bars—in these images. This gave rise to a giant map of the internal structure of galaxies. The intriguing question: can this approach also spot ring-shaped forms among them, as rare as a five-petaled flower?

1

An ordinary photo editor highlights objects in an image. The ZooBot:3D neural network works with distant galaxies like a cartographer drawing a map of an unexplored land. Thousands of volunteers manually traced spiral arms and bars (central stellar bands) on images, and from these lessons the algorithm learned to create not just lines, but heatmaps: each pixel tells whether an arm is there.

Surprise: the network started finding rings — rare circular structures. In the training examples, there were almost none. The algorithm seemed to figure out on its own what these 'donuts' look like.

With such maps, astronomers match data on stellar motion (spectroscopy) and brightness (photometry) to understand galaxy structure tens of times faster. The resulting catalog of 639,636 galaxies is open and already combined with the MaNGA and SAMI projects.

🎯 Volunteers of the Galaxy Zoo project manually traced the outlines of thousands of galaxies, making millions of annotations. Now a single run of the neural network replaces this titanic effort.

🎬 Mapping galaxies is a key motif in science fiction, from Asimov’s 'Foundation' to 'Star Wars'; this work takes a step toward a real cosmic atlas.
