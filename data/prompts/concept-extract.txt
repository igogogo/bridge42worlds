DEFINITION — what counts as a CONCEPT (apply strictly):
A concept is a named, reusable unit of scientific knowledge that
  (a) has an accepted name used by the field, not invented in this one paper;
  (b) can be defined in one sentence independently of this paper;
  (c) a curious reader would plausibly want explained.
NOT a concept: paper-specific model names, one-off constructions, authors' ad-hoc
composites. When in doubt ask: would a second, unrelated paper use this same name?
If no — skip it.
